2 types of lookup's in ADF
1.lookup activity
2.lookup transformation---->in dataflows

Limitations of lookup activity

1.5000,4mb,24hrs
  It can read small volumes-->upto 5000rows(so everytime lookup activity 
  cannot work)

2)Data volume should not exceed 4mb(i.e max limit 4mb)

3)Runtime execution timeout---->24hrs max limit
  if your pipeline activity exceeds more than 24hrs, pipeline will be 
  automatically discarded
   
  ex:  data <4mb only , but no of recs=6000--->It wont fail--->it reads 5000rows
       if rows<5000 ,but data>4mb------>then it will failed


      This is version1 style
      In Version2---->we got dataflows---->lookup transformation

   transformations and then write into sql or cosmosdb------->
   ------>so here apply copy activity
   ------>keep data in datalake
   ------>now apply dataflows
   ------>within dataflows---->we have many transformations
   ------>using transformations process data--->write into target
