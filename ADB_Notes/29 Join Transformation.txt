
Join Transformation:
-------------------

Types of merging: 2 types
1.Vertical merging: merging of rows vertically
  ex: union
  Condition for vertical merging: Schema of the merging datasets should be same

2.Horizontal merging :Merging of cols horizontally
  ex:joins
  Condition for join :Atleast one column should be common in both datasets

==============================================================================
Types of joins:3 types
1.Inner join
2.Outer Join------>3types---->1)Left outer join
                              2)Right outer join
                              3)full outer join
3.cross Join

A=1   B=1
  2     2
  3     3
  4     7
  5     8
  6     9

1)Inner join: only matching records (intersection)
o/p:
     (1,1)
     (2,2)
     (3,3)

2)Left outer join: Matchings+unmatched of left side
                             i.e total presence of left side

o/p:
     (1,1)
     (2,2)
     (3,3)
     (4, )
     (5, )
     (6, )

3)Right outer join: Matchings+unmatched of Right side
                             i.e total presence of Right side

o/p:
     (1,1)
     (2,2)
     (3,3)
     ( ,7)
     ( ,8)
     ( ,9)

4)Full outer join: Matchings+unmatched of left side
                             i.e total presence of left side
                            +unmatched of Right side
                             i.e total presence of Right side

o/p:
     (1,1)
     (2,2)
     (3,3)
     (4, )
     (5, )
     (6, )
     ( ,7)
     ( ,8)
     ( ,9)

5)cross join :Each element of leftside merges with each element of right side

A=(a,b,c)
B=(1,2,3)

A X B=(a,1),(a,2),(a,3),
      (b,1),(b,2),(b,3)
      (c,1),(c,2),(c,3)
