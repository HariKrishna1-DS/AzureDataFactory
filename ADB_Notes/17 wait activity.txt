
wait Activity:
-------------

wait means--------->configuring waiting time(in secs)
                    before executing the next activity

Here we see 2 advantages

when records are loaded into database,commit happens, a ACK goes to source App,
here the activity got executed but there must be some delay in getting ACK
from the target, that's why

1)activity1(load)---->wait(10sec)---->Activity2(filter)

 between these 2 activities, i will configure some waiting time
 how much time to be configured?---->it depends on the previous tracking or
 the behavior of the target database

during pipeline execution some activities are expecting some ACK from other 
system,at that time the process will be delayed,
without getting that ACK,if next activity starts executing, there is a 
high chance of failure of activity
so when activities are expecting some ACK, so include wait Activity.

2)To test whether a logic is working perfectly or not

  ex: if-condition-->check whther it is woking fine or not

      To validate the condition
      wait1------->we give under True part---->10secs
      wait2------->we give under False part--->20secs

  Once it is validating fine, then you can configure the actual activity

  ex:  if-condition
           Activities---->some condition is provided
                 True---->Edit---->search:wait1
                                          settings 
                                          wait:10secs

                 False--->Edit---->search:wait2
                                          settings
                                          wait:20secs

      If-condition perfectly configured

       ur observation---->wait1 executed means----->if-condition is True
                          wait2 executed means----->if-condition is False
 
                         if it is working fine then remove
                         wait1 and wait2 and configure the other activities
