
Pivot Transformation:
---------------------
pivot T/R--->creating cols from rows

means creating multiple columns from the unique row values of a single column

-Pivot is a aggregation T/R where you can select group by columns and generate
 pivot columns using aggregate functions

//emp.csv
Eid	Ename	Salary	sex	dno	city
101	Miller	10000	m	11	hyd
102	Blake	20000	m	12	pune
103	Sony	30000	f	11	hyd
104	Sita	40000	f	12	pune
105	John	50000	m	13	hyd

in the above dataset--->take a column city---> 
in the city column->we have 5 rows->from this unique rows of city generate cols

here city has 2 unique vlaues--->i.e hyd,pune--->
so generate 2 cols --->with names hyd and pune 

and here group by col-->dno
aggregation(count) on col-> eid

creating multiple columns from the unique row values

o/p: dno   hyd   pune
     11     2     0(NULL)
     12     0     2
     13     1     0

sql query for the above:
select dno,count(distinct eid) from emp group by dno
