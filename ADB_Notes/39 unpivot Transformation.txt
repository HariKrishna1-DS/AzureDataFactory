
unpivot Tranformation:
---------------------
opposite to pivot Transformation

pivot==>converting row values into col values

unpivot=>converting col values into Row values

pivot===> Row------> columns
unpivot==>column---> Rows

ex: 
  ex:     city         id  dno   hyd  pune
          hyd   pivot   1  11    2    1    unpivot
          pune=======>  2  12    1    2===========>o/p we get how many rows-6
          hyd           3  13    1    1
          pune
          hyd

      id   dno   city  emp_count
       1    11   hyd      2
       1    11   pune     1
       2    12   hyd      1
       2    12   pune     2
       3    13   hyd      1
       3    13   pune     1
