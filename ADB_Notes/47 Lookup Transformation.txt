
Lookup Transformation:
----------------------
Lookup Transformation is similar to left outer join

left outer join :matchings+unmatched recs of leftside

All rows from the primary stream will exist in the o/p with additional cols
from the lookup upstream

ex: 
 left table      Right table
total data of lefttable+matched data of Right table
